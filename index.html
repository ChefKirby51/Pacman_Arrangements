What is Pac-man Arrangement?


Pac-man Arrangement is a remake of the original pac-man, originally as part of the Namco Classic Collection Volume 2 arcade cabinat back in 1996.
Pac-man Arrangement was also available in the Gameboy Advance's Pac-man Collection in 2001, different Namco Museum ports (GameCube, PS2, Xbox and PSP), as well as the Pac-man Museum+ for PC and all modern consoles.


How do you play Pac-man Arrangement?


Arrangement works about the same as your typical Pac-man game goes:
*Eat all the pac pellets in a level to win
*Avoid the ghosts who are chasing you
*Collect fruit for extra points
*Collect power pellets to eat ghosts
Except this time, the ghosts have some new tricks up their sleeves.

Think you can beat it?


Arrangement might be pretty old, but it still has some classic arcade difficulty and some unique systems that will keep you on your toes.
Luckily, This site provides info and tips on everything about the game. As well as some activities for those who wish to play the game themselves.
Good Luck and have fun!
