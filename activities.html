Speed Running Tips:

  • While getting through levels is important, going for ghost eating comboes takes presidence, especially when ghosts power up. So you are playing high-risk, high-reward.
  • Red and Blue Pills help you eat ghost, fruit and pellets at a faster, safer rate.
