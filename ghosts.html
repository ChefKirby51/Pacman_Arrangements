Know you Enemy!

Unlike the ghosts in previous games, and honestly any pac-man game afterwards, Blinky, Pinky, Inky and Clyde can now transform into upgraded versions of themselves thanks to in part by their mysterious new 5th gang-member Kinky. Not only does the ghost gang have new abilities, but said abilities can be used against them with items of which can be explained in on the items page. Here, you will learn not only how to properly avoid these pac hungery monster, but use them to your advantage.

    Ghost Bot

  • In the second part of the Final Battle between the Ghost Gang, You
    face off against the Ghost Bot, who will chase you while you eat green
    pellets everywhere on the stage.
  • As you eat pellets, the bot will begin gaining speed and, with enough
    pellets eaten, will go haywire and start bouncing from wall to wall,
    aimlessly. During this time, it's best to watch the Ghost Bot's
    trajectory as you eat more pellets. Once all eaten, grab the Pac-man
    object on the maze. And, with that, you win!
